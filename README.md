Cayo Verde — an original open-world crime game set in a sun-soaked island city. Play Dani Vega, a driver in debt to the fixer Sal Marín, across five story missions: fares, repo runs, hot deliveries, a ledger heist, and one last bridge out. Explore the city on foot or behind the wheel, steal and drive cars, dodge a five-star police response, repaint your ride at the spray shop, fight with fists and a pistol, and hunt ten hidden sun medallions. Traffic, pedestrians, a live minimap, synthesized engine and siren audio, and save/resume are all built in. Every name, place, and story beat is original, and the whole game is static files — no build step, no network requests.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open the printed URL in a browser. Controls: WASD to move/drive, mouse to steer the camera, F to enter/exit cars and talk, 1/2 for fists/pistol, click to attack, Shift to sprint, P to pause, M to mute.
